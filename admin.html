Existing users

A Clinical Team Lead can only hide an unused account (reversible, list-only — but it does also revoke access immediately). A Developer can additionally delete an account permanently: access is revoked for good and their password invalidated, though leave history stays on record. Developer accounts can never be suspended, hidden, or deleted from the app by anyone — that's Supabase-dashboard-only. A Clinical Team Lead additionally can't reset the password or edit the site of another Clinical Team Lead or a Developer.

Import historical leave

Developer only. Manually back-fills a leave record from an old spreadsheet or paper record — set who it's for, who requested it, who approved it (or the rejection reason), and the original dates. This bypasses the normal self-only/approval-flow rules that apply to everyone else, since it's recreating something that already happened rather than raising something new. The same one-request-per-day check still applies, so it won't silently create a duplicate for a date that's already on record.

Times are stored as entered in your browser's local time zone — worth a quick sanity check on the calendar afterwards if precise timing matters for a TOIL record.

Reset app data for go-live

Developer only. Permanently deletes every leave request, bank holiday, restricted day, capacity override, and Clinical Rota Completed / DSR complete flag, resets each site's leave-capacity threshold back to its default, and clears the audit log — so the app starts completely clean for real use. User accounts, roles, and site assignments are not touched — nobody needs to be recreated. This cannot be undone.

Confirm: Reset App Data

This permanently deletes all leave requests, bank holidays, restricted days, capacity overrides, and month-complete flags, and clears the audit log. User accounts are kept exactly as they are. There is no way to undo this. Enter your password to confirm.
