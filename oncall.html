Swapping a shift? Hold Ctrl (or Cmd on a Mac) and click one or more of your own shifts, then click one or more shifts belonging to one colleague, then press Request swap. You must include at least one of your own shifts. On a phone or tablet use Select shifts to swap instead.

1. Rota period

The rota is built inside a named period (usually a year). Pick it in the “Rota period” box above, or start a new one.

2. Pharmacist colours

Each pharmacist's shifts are shown in their own colour on the rota. Click a colour to change it.

3. Bank holidays

Mark which days are bank holidays for the on-call rota (separate from the leave calendar's). A bank holiday is shown in the colour you pick (light blue by default) and flags if its onsite shift is still unfilled.

4. Quick build

Choose a pharmacist, then paste their dates in the format DD/MM/YYYY (one per line, or separated by spaces or commas). Their name goes into every one of those slots. On a Saturday, Sunday or bank holiday the Onsite slot is filled too. Repeat for each pharmacist, then fine-tune single days in the grid above.
