Pending Approvals

Every pending request — Annual Leave, TOIL, or Study Leave — is numbered in the order it was submitted. A Lead Pharmacist can only approve/reject a Pharmacist's leave (never their own, another Lead Pharmacist's, or a Clinical Team Lead's); a Clinical Team Lead can approve Pharmacist or Lead Pharmacist leave, or their own — a Developer can action anyone's. A ⚠ Restricted day badge means the request falls on a day that's either auto-flagged for leave capacity or explicitly marked restricted for that site.
